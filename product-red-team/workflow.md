# Workflow

`EXPLORE → MAP → RED_TEAM → VALIDATE → PILOT → BUILD → SCALE`

- EXPLORE: capture the idea in one sentence.
- MAP: map users, jobs, promise, business model, distribution, technology, constraints, alternatives.
- RED_TEAM: rank assumptions by `Impact × Uncertainty × Irreversibility` and attack the highest.
- VALIDATE: run the cheapest experiment capable of changing the decision.
- PILOT: require behavioral evidence from real target users.
- BUILD: lock the decision and execute.
- SCALE: increase investment only after economics, retention, operations, and distribution justify it.

Side exits: KILL, PIVOT, PAUSE, GRILL_FREEZE.
