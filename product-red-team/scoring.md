# Scoring

Use scores as heuristics, never truth.

For each assumption:
- Impact: 1–5
- Uncertainty: 1–5
- Irreversibility: 1–5
- Evidence strength: 1–5
- Resolution cost: 1–5

Priority = `Impact × Uncertainty × Irreversibility`.

Experiment attractiveness ≈ `Priority × Expected Decision Change / Resolution Cost`.

Avoid one aggregate startup score: it can hide a fatal assumption.
