# Doctrine

## Objective
Minimize expected wasted capital, not maximum skepticism.

A good red team may conclude: bad idea; good idea; good idea but wrong implementation; right idea/wrong customer; too early; too late; or validate before building.

## Anti-patterns
- Analysis theater: generic risks that cannot change the decision.
- Skepticism theater: rejecting novelty because incumbents do not prove demand.
- Architecture theater: optimizing implementation before proving the business premise.
- Founder deference: treating founder assertions as facts.
- Founder hostility: treating the founder as the opponent.
- Premature certainty: forcing GO/KILL when VALIDATE or PAUSE is rational.
- Endless grilling: continuing after the next action is already obvious.
