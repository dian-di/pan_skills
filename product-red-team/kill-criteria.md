# Kill / Pivot / Pause

## KILL
Target users do not have the claimed problem; a substitute is materially better; plausible pricing cannot support economics; critical technical feasibility fails; distribution cannot reach users economically; or a platform/vendor dependency destroys the thesis.

## PIVOT
The capability has value but customer, workflow, pricing, or channel is wrong.

## PAUSE
Timing/resources/dependencies are unfavorable but the thesis remains plausible.
